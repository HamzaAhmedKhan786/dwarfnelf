# Domain Data Governance
Pack manifests record license, attribution, official publisher, allowed download/redistribution, geography, language, effective date, version, hash and update channel. Medical and legal content require authority tiers and date-aware retrieval. User documents remain local; indexing must support ACLs, revocation, source deletion propagation, provenance and backups. Publicly accessible does not imply freely redistributable. Never ship copyrighted/restricted corpora without rights. Use source-verified downloadable manifests rather than hardcoded unverified links.
